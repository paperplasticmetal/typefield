# Tour and icon refinement — 0.59.20 (100)

The welcome specimen says Hello, has no decorative dot beside the wordmark, and compares fonts using their actual lowercase-x ink at one common x-height and baseline. Word outlines remain uniformly scaled and horizontally centered; the three fonts keep their own natural spacing and proportions. The initial Library specimen carries Hello forward.

Each tour step has one relevant workspace shortcut: Browse Fonts, Open Spaces, or Draw a Letter. Finish tour dismisses the final step. The Letterforms page includes brief definitions of baseline, x-height, cap height, ascenders, descenders, side bearings and advance, with three matching guide lines beside the example.

The curve demo has a solid 2D anchor and two independently draggable Bézier handles. Moving the anchor translates both handles; each hollow handle controls its actual adjacent cubic. Bounds keep this disposable example usable and its counter open. A point picker and directional buttons provide an alternative to dragging. Reset restores all three points, and geometry survives page navigation.

Optional Dock icons now share the existing upright monogram and differ only in color: Porcelain, Ochre, Indigo, Sage, Plum and Coral. Coral uses the website’s ink/coral pair. Textures, decorative guides, rotations and relief effects are removed. Stored preference IDs and app accent colors are preserved. Porcelain and the bundled app icon are unchanged.
