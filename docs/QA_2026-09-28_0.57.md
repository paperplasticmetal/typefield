# Typefield 0.57 diagonal refinement

The requested 85% target remains unmet. The app's suggestions are still a deterministic, provisional vector construction, and silhouette IoU is not calibrated confidence.

## Retained change

Eligible K/M/N reuse vertical stems and terminals from the supplied H. New diagonals use its measured stem weight. K and N follow H's physical ink width, preventing a broader O from spreading K's diagonals. M moves the two stems apart without stretching their weight. Monospaced M keeps its previous internal construction, which performed better in the controlled trial. Rounded/irregular or substantially leaning H drawings retain the existing fallback.

The source is preserved; results are independent closed vectors. New tests check H provenance, bounded editing anchors, physical placement and width, invariance to a broader O, monospaced advances, source preservation, ablation and TrueType export.

## Same-protocol measurements

Five references remain H O n o p, with all 47 withheld letters counted per face. Failed suggestions would count as zero. No target glyph participates in inference. The nine-style mean rises from **52.3% to 52.9%**, or **53.0%** at the 368 × 288 grid. The original five-style mean is approximately **58.7%**. Threshold coverage is **34/423** letters at least 85% on the original grid, **35/423** at the finer grid. Worst-style overlap remains 16.3%.

## Rejected experiments

- Reusing compressed O halves for B/P/R regressed the aggregate. A nonlinear vertical mapping preserved more weight but made some serif bowls pointed. Both were rejected.
- The official learned-vector checkpoint, after compact-reference, contour-convention, fill-rule and reference-only calibration checks, remained poor. Its additional soft curve refinement also regressed.
- A reference-fitted raster mixture with smooth deformation reached 65.6% in its separate 128 × 128 experiment. It has not passed editable-vector validation and is not shipped.
- Local kernel predictors trained on explicitly open-licensed fonts reached approximately 60–63% across the two font sets, with poor script results. Family-based model selection, target-lineage exclusion and frozen test predictions were used. Rasterization and geometry handling differ from the production audit, so these are not app accuracy figures.

Research protocol details and limitations are in [the research notes](../scripts/starter-research/README.md). No model runtime, downloaded weight, borrowed font outline or training dataset is added to the app. Temporary/private font artifacts remain outside version control.
